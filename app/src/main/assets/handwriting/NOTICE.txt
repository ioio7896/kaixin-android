The upstream model card reports top-1 95.47% / top-10 99.58% for its evaluation.

This 3755-class model is a simplified single-character classifier. The app also
bundles a separate extended model for native traditional/rare glyphs and line
recognition; see extended/NOTICE.txt. Output script conversion uses OpenCC.
